Our story

A small family kitchen that grew into a place for gatherings.

From home kitchen to dining hall

Saryarka Kitchen opened in 2014 with six tables and one idea: serve the food people remember from their grandmother's house. Today we still cook in small batches and make our dough, noodles and bread by hand every day.

In Kazakh culture a guest is a gift. We try to welcome every visitor that way.

What we promise

  • Fresh ingredients, never frozen meat
  • Halal kitchen
  • Vegetarian dishes on every menu section
  • Seats for large family groups
